# Otavio Vasconcelos

**Software Engineer · Product & AI Builder · Founder**

I build products at the intersection of software, AI, and business. I'm the founder
and technical lead of **Loja Veículos**, a B2B ERP for vehicle dealerships in Brazil,
and previously the **first engineering hire at Venda.ai** (AI retail sales agent) and
at **LootRush** (US-based startup, later raised $12M+ seed). I like owning the whole
problem: product decisions, architecture, frontend, backend, data, infra, and the
customer feedback loop.

## What I work on

- **0 → 1 product engineering** — taking ambiguous, real-world problems to production systems with paying users
- **AI in production** — LLM agents with tool calling, human-in-the-loop confirmation, cost/latency discipline, and evaluation — not demos
- **Full-stack systems** — TypeScript (React, Node/Hono), Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL, Docker, Railway/AWS
